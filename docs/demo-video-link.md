# CareCircle Demo Video

## Video Script Outline (5 minutes max)

### 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
- "CareCircle is an AI-powered breast cancer screening coordination agent"
- Show the problem: screening gaps, missed appointments, complex care coordination
- "Built with AWS Bedrock Strands Agents"

### 2. The Problem (45 seconds)
- Breast cancer is the most common cancer in women worldwide
- Early detection improves 5-year survival from 28% to 99%
- But 33% of eligible women are NOT up to date on screenings
- Care coordination is fragmented and confusing for patients
- Risk assessment is underutilized in primary care

### 3. Who It's For (30 seconds)
- Patients navigating screening decisions
- Healthcare providers managing patient populations
- Care coordinators ensuring screening compliance
- Health systems improving early detection rates

### 4. Why It Matters (30 seconds)
- Every missed screening is a missed chance at early detection
- AI can personalize screening plans and remove barriers
- Automated coordination reduces administrative burden
- Better adherence = more lives saved

### 5. Live Demo (2.5 minutes)
- Show Streamlit Dashboard
- Run Risk Assessment with sample patient
- Show risk score visualization and breakdown
- Schedule a mammogram screening
- Generate personalized care plan
- Show education center content
- Demonstrate AI chat interaction
- Show API documentation

### 6. Architecture & Tech (30 seconds)
- AWS Bedrock + Strands Agents framework
- Custom tools for each capability
- FastAPI backend, Streamlit frontend
- Evidence-based risk model (modified Gail Model)

### 7. Closing (15 seconds)
- "CareCircle: AI-powered care that doesn't replace your doctor, it helps you get to them on time"
- Open source, MIT licensed
- Call to action

## Recording Tips
- Use OBS or similar for screen recording
- Record at 1080p minimum
- Add voiceover narration
- Use the Streamlit demo for visual appeal
- Show the CLI demo output for technical credibility
- Include the architecture diagram
